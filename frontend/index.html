How CampusLoop works

A transparent 3-step process from issue discovery to verified resolution.

01

Snap & Pin

Capture a photo of the problem and optionally drop a pin within the SIES GST Nerul campus boundary. Location is always optional.

02

Admin Action

Administrators review the submission with department records, trust scores, and exact coordinates to dispatch maintenance personnel.

03

Photo-Proof Fix

The issue is marked resolved only when the team uploads a photo of the completed repair for the entire campus to view.
